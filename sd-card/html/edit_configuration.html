Configuration
Device, recognition and integration settings.
How it works

Topics

Settings are grouped into General, Network, Recognition and MQTT. Rarely needed options appear when you switch on Advanced settings.

Descriptions

Hover over or click the question mark next to a setting to read what it does.

Saving

As soon as you change something, a save bar appears at the bottom. If a change needs a restart, it says Save and restart. Discard drops your changes.
