Setup
Reference image, alignment markers and the digit and analog regions of your meter.
How it works

Image

Capture a new reference image with your camera settings or keep the current one. Make sure it is level (use Image Rotation). Markers and ROIs are placed on this image.

Markers

Two markers let the device correct small shifts and rotations. Pick a marker, drag its area on the image or enter coordinates, then click Update Marker. Keep markers small, sharp and high-contrast.

Digits and analog

Group ROIs (Regions Of Interest) into number sequences. Drag them or enter coordinates; their order defines the decimal place, Move higher / lower changes it. See the ROI documentation.

Saving

Markers, digits and analog share one save bar at the bottom. A new image is saved separately on the Image tab.
